Oracle Corporation is offering $500,000,000 aggregate principal amount of floating rate notes due 2029 (the “Floating Rate Notes”), $3,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 4.550% notes due 2029 (the “2029 Fixed Rate Notes”), $3,500,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 4.950% notes due 2031 (the “2031 Fixed Rate Notes”), $3,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.350% notes due 2033 (the “2033 Fixed Rate Notes”), $5,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.700% notes due 2036 (the “2036 Fixed Rate Notes”), $2,250,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 6.550% notes due 2046 (the “2046 Fixed Rate Notes”), $5,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 6.700% notes due 2056 (the “2056 Fixed Rate Notes”) and $2,750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 6.850% notes due 2066 (the “2066 Fixed Rate Notes” and, together with the 2029 Fixed Rate Notes, the 2031 Fixed Rate Notes, the 2033 Fixed Rate Notes, the 2036 Fixed Rate Notes, the 2046 Fixed Rate Notes and the 2056 Fixed Rate Notes, the “Fixed Rate Notes”). We refer to the Floating Rate Notes and the Fixed Rate Notes collectively as the “Notes.”

The Floating Rate Notes will bear interest at a floating rate equal to Compounded SOFR (as defined herein), plus 1.11% per year. Interest on the Floating Rate Notes will be payable quarterly on February 4, May 4, August 4 and November 4, commencing May 4, 2026.

The 2029 Fixed Rate Notes will bear interest at the rate of 4.550% per year, the 2031 Fixed Rate Notes will bear interest at the rate of 4.950% per year, the 2033 Fixed Rate Notes will bear interest at the rate of 5.350% per year, the 2036 Fixed Rate Notes will bear interest at the rate of 5.700% per year, the 2046 Fixed Rate Notes will bear interest at the rate of 6.550% per year, the 2056 Fixed Rate Notes will bear interest at the rate of 6.700% per year and the 2066 Fixed Rate Notes will bear interest at the rate of 6.850% per year. Interest on the 2029 Fixed Rate Notes, the 2031 Fixed Rate Notes, the 2036 Fixed Rate Notes, the 2046 Fixed Rate Notes, the 2056 Fixed Rate Notes and the 2066 Fixed Rate Notes will be payable semi-annually on February 4 and August 4, commencing August 4, 2026. Interest on the 2033 Fixed Rate Notes will be payable semi-annually on May 4 and November 4, commencing November 4, 2026.

The Floating Rate Notes will mature on February 4, 2029, the 2029 Fixed Rate Notes will mature on February 4, 2029, the 2031 Fixed Rate Notes will mature on February 4, 2031, the 2033 Fixed Rate Notes will mature on May 4, 2033, the 2036 Fixed Rate Notes will mature on February 4, 2036, the 2046 Fixed Rate Notes will mature on February 4, 2046, the 2056 Fixed Rate Notes will mature on February 4, 2056 and the 2066 Fixed Rate Notes will mature on February 4, 2066.

We may not redeem the Floating Rate Notes prior to maturity. We may redeem the Fixed Rate Notes of any series, in whole or in part, at any time, each at the applicable redemption prices indicated under the heading “Description of the Notes—Optional Redemption” beginning on page S-22 of this prospectus supplement. The Notes will rank equally with all of our other existing and future unsecured and unsubordinated indebtedness from time to time outstanding.

Concurrently with this offering and pursuant to a separate prospectus supplement, we are making a public offering of 100,000,000 depositary shares, each of which represents a 1/2,000th interest in a share of our 6.50% Series D Mandatory Convertible Preferred Stock (the “Depositary Shares Offering”). In addition, concurrently with this offering and pursuant to a separate prospectus supplement, we are entering into an Equity Distribution Agreement with certain agents under which we may sell up to $20 billion of shares of our common stock in “at-the-market” transactions from time to time (the “ATM Program” and, together with the Depositary Shares Offering, the “Concurrent Financing Transactions”). The closing of this offering is not conditioned upon the closing of the Depositary Shares Offering or entry into the ATM Program. Neither the closing of the Depositary Shares Offering nor entry into the ATM Program is conditioned upon the closing of this offering.
