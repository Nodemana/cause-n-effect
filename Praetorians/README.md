# Praetorians

Praetorians critique theses, and that is their only job. After the Centurions write individual theses, the Legate merges linked theses. A thesis the Legate does not merge gets no second pass and comes straight here from that first thesis. A merged finding comes here only after a Centurion writes a second, fresh full thesis on that merged lead. Critique comes before the Legate ranks.

They receive the Centurion's output: the recommendation (where to put the money, the expected return, the risks, and the timeframe) and the stated assumptions. They have no assumptions section. The assumptions under attack are the Centurion's.

On any prior price run, the Centurion has already classified it as a new thesis, an acceleration of an existing thesis, or tail end / likely priced in, with reasoning and evidence, and noted that call as a concern where relevant. The Praetorians review that classification and state whether they agree or disagree, with counter-evidence. The Centurion decides first. The Praetorians challenge that call.

They challenge:

- An assumption that does not make sense, and why.
- Research taken from a bad source, or from any source other than an SEC filing, an ASX announcement, or a company release.
- Further research that contradicts the Centurion's findings.

They argue the other side of every claim. Counter-evidence is aimed at the Centurion's stated assumptions, sources, and conclusions. They do not merge leads, and they do not rank.
