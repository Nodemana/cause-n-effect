# debt_raise

## Criteria

- min_market_cap: market cap is at least the threshold stored on the rubric.
- raise_vs_company: the stated raise is at least the threshold percent of market cap.
- specific_proceeds: proceeds name spending, an acquisition, or a project, rather than general corporate purposes.

## Grade

Grade this lead only, against each criterion above. Pass or fail, with the figure and its source.

## Thesis and ROI

Research this lead only. State how a return on investment can actually be achieved, and the potential ROI.
Name what the proceeds fund. A general corporate purposes line is not a program.
Carry the lead through to one conclusion.

## Timeframe

Estimate how long this thesis should take to play out.

## Risks

Set out the risks.

## Recommendation

Name the instrument or ticker, the expected return, the risks, and the timeframe. This thesis is for the Imperator.

## Assumptions

List every assumption made while researching and forming the thesis.

Figures only from SEC filings, ASX announcements, and company releases.

If this lead is the single new lead from a Legate merge, this is the second pass: still write one grade, one thesis, one ROI, one set of risks, one timeframe, and one assumptions list. A lead that was not merged does not reach this pass.

On that second pass, classify any prior price run as a new thesis, an acceleration of an existing thesis, or tail end / likely priced in, with reasoning and evidence. Note that call as a concern where relevant. There is no automatic rejection.

On that second pass, macro context is a Centurion concern. Read the Macro scout cache when it is supplied. Do not re-pull those series. If no cache is supplied, do not invent macro conditions.
